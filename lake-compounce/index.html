Day by Day

The Itinerary

1Day
Day One
Drive out, set up camp & ride the coasters
  • 9:00 AM
    Depart Albany Drive
    Hit the road at 9 AM for Lake Compounce (~2.5–3 hrs). Directions from Albany ↗
  • ~12:00 PM
    Set up the tents 📍 Camp
    Check in at the campground, pitch the tents, and get the site sorted before the fun starts.
  • Afternoon
    Amusement park with the kids 📍 Fun
    Turn the kids loose at Lake Compounce — coasters, the classic Wildcat, the trolley, and Crocodile Cove water park.
    🎟️ ~$45–55 per person at the gate (cheaper online; kids/juniors discounted). 🕐 Opens ~11 AM; hours are seasonal — check the calendar. Tickets & hours ↗
  • Evening
    Grill & mutton by the fire Food
    Back at camp — fire up the grill for a mutton feast, unwind around the campfire, and let the kids toast marshmallows.
2Day
Day Two
Camp breakfast, pack up & a stop on the way home
  • Morning
    Breakfast in the camp Food
    A relaxed camp breakfast — coffee on the fire, eggs and leftovers from last night.
  • By 12:00 PM
    Wrap up the tents & check out Camp
    Pack up the tents, clear the site, and be on the road by noon.
  • Afternoon
    Nearby sightseeing Explore
    Break up the drive with a stop near Bristol: New England Carousel Museum 📍 · American Clock & Watch Museum 📍 · Heublein Tower / Talcott Mountain 📍 (scenic hike) · Mark Twain House, Hartford 📍.
  • Night
    Reach Albany Drive
    Cruise back home (~2.5–3 hrs) and roll into Albany by night — tired kids, full hearts, and a phone full of photos.
