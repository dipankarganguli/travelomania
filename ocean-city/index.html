Around Town

Nearby Attractions

Boardwalk
Ocean City Boardwalk 📍
~3 mi of shops, arcades, and Thrasher's fries — right downtown.
Beach
Ocean City Beach 📍
Wide free beach — bundle up, it's late-November brisk.
Rides
Trimper's Rides 📍 · Jolly Roger 📍
Classic boardwalk amusements — check winter hours, many rides are seasonal.
Wild horses
Assateague Island 📍
Wild ponies & dunes, ~15 min south. Great cold-weather day trip.
Park
Northside Park 📍
Bayfront park & walking paths (Winterfest of Lights nearby in season).
Museum
Life-Saving Station Museum 📍
Small local-history museum at the south end of the Boardwalk.

Indian food & groceries

Indian options right in Ocean City are limited; the best bets are a short drive west toward Salisbury (~30 min).

🍽️ Restaurants: Indian restaurants in Ocean City 📍 · Indian restaurants in Salisbury 📍
🛒 Groceries / stores: Indian grocery in Salisbury 📍 · nearest Indian grocery 📍

ℹ️ Late November is Ocean City's off-season — call ahead, as some restaurants, rides, and attractions keep reduced winter hours or close for the season.
