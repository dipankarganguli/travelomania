Day by Day

The Itinerary

🍽️ Food strategy (no beef or pork): lunch at the attractions each day — the Art Institute café (Wed), the Field Bistro (Thu), Giordano's vegetarian deep-dish at Navy Pier (Fri), and a spot near the Loop (Sat) — with Indian Uber Eats delivered to the hotel most evenings (paneer, dal, chana masala, veg biryani, tandoori chicken, naan). It keeps sightseeing time free and side-steps beef/pork entirely.
1Day
Wednesday, August 19
Arrival · Art Institute · The Bean · River Cruise
  • 8:10 AM
    United UA 214 departs Albany (ALB) Travel
    Non-stop to O'Hare. Get to ALB ~2 hrs early — 6 travelers, so allow time at security.
  • 9:45 AM
    Arrive O'Hare (ORD) 📍 Travel
    Collect luggage ~10:15–10:30 AM.
  • 10:30–11:30 AM
    UberXXL / 2 Ubers → Travelodge 📍 Travel
    Drop the bags at the front desk (before official check-in) so you can start right away.
  • 11:40 AM
    Walk to the Art Institute 📍 Culture
    ~10 min walk. Enter (~11:50 AM) and ask staff about Fullerton Hall.
  • ~12:30 PM
    ⭐ Fullerton Hall / Vivekananda site 📍 Culture
    If accessible — the hall tied to Swami Vivekananda's 1893 Parliament of Religions address.
  • 12:45–1:30 PM
    Lunch at the Art Institute café Food
    Use the museum's dining/café — pick vegetarian, chicken or fish rather than squeezing in a separate restaurant. (No beef/pork.)
  • 1:30–2:50 PM
    Art Institute highlights + Vivekananda plaque 📍 Culture
    The Impressionist wing and American Gothic, then the Vivekananda plaque nearby.
  • 2:50–3:25 PM
    ⭐ Millennium Park + The Bean 📍 Views
    Millennium Park 📍, Cloud Gate, and Crown Fountain. Great for group photos.
  • 3:40–4:25 PM
    Check in & freshen up Stay
    Walk back to the hotel (~15 min), check in, and rest before the cruise.
  • 4:25–5:30 PM
    UberXL → Navy Pier 📍 Travel
    Arrive ~4:50, restroom, find the boarding point, and be at the cruise boarding area by 5:30.
  • 5:45–7:00 PM
    🚢 Architecture River Cruise 📍 Tour
    90 minutes on the river — the best way to see the skyline. Reserve tickets in advance. UberXL back to the hotel after (~7:15).
  • 8:30–9:00 PM
    🍽️ Uber Eats dinner at the hotel Food
    Easiest for six with the no-beef/pork preference: Indian — dal, paneer, chana masala, tandoori chicken, biryani, naan. Pizza also works (cheese / veg / chicken; confirm no pork topping).
2Day
Thursday, August 20
Field Museum · Shedd Aquarium · Buckingham Fountain
  • 7:30–8:15 AM
    Breakfast Food
    Fuel up before a big museum day.
  • 8:30 AM
    UberXL → Field Museum 📍 Travel
    On the Museum Campus 📍 by the lake.
  • 9:00 AM–12:00 PM
    🦖 Field Museum 📍 Culture
    SUE the T. rex and the main halls — a full, kid-friendly morning.
  • 12:00–12:45 PM
    🍽️ Lunch at the Field Bistro Food
    On-site — vegetarian sandwich/wrap, salad, chicken, or flatbread. Avoid anything with bacon, ham, sausage, pepperoni or beef. Eat here before walking to Shedd so you get an uninterrupted block there.
  • 12:45–1:00 PM
    Walk to the Shedd Aquarium 📍 Walk
    A short walk across the Museum Campus.
  • 1:00–5:00 PM
    🐠 Shedd Aquarium 📍 Culture
    A full four-hour block — aquatic show, the Caribbean Reef, and the Oceanarium.
  • 5:00–6:30 PM
    UberXL → hotel & rest Stay
    Back to the hotel to recharge before the evening stroll.
  • 6:45–7:30 PM
    ⭐ Buckingham Fountain + Grant Park 📍 Views
    Grant Park 📍 and the fountain, a short walk from the hotel. Walk back ~7:45 and relax.
  • 8:30–9:00 PM
    🍽️ Uber Eats dinner at the hotel Food
    Indian is easiest for the group — a mix of vegetarian and chicken (paneer, dal, chana masala, veg biryani, tandoori chicken, naan).
3Day
Friday, August 21
Navy Pier · FlyOver · Ohio Street Beach · Skydeck
  • 8:00–9:00 AM
    Breakfast Food
    Then UberXL to Navy Pier at 9:45 AM.
  • 10:15–11:30 AM
    🎡 Navy Pier 📍 Fun
    Lakefront walk and the pier. Optional: the Centennial Wheel 📍 (~$20/person).
  • 11:30 AM–12:15 PM
    ✈️ FlyOver Chicago 📍 Fun
    Right at Navy Pier — a flight-simulation ride over Chicago's skyline (wind, mist, motion). ~$28 per person (approx.; check current pricing).
  • 12:30–1:30 PM
    🍕 Lunch: Giordano's at Navy Pier 📍 Food
    Try Chicago deep dish without changing your diet — order a vegetarian deep-dish (cheese, spinach, mushroom, peppers, onions). Easy, right on the pier.
  • 2:00–3:10 PM
    🏖️ Ohio Street Beach 📍 Beach
    Walk over from the pier (~30 min) for a relaxed hour+ on the sand with the skyline behind you.
  • 3:30–4:15 PM
    ☕ Starbucks Reserve Roastery 📍 Fun
    The 5-story Roastery on Michigan Ave. Optional: Garrett Popcorn 📍 next door.
  • 5:00–6:30 PM
    ⭐ Hotel rest / shower / change Stay
    UberXL back to the hotel to recharge before the Skydeck.
  • 7:30–~9:00 PM
    🌇 Willis Tower Skydeck + The Ledge 📍 Views
    UberXL over (~6:45 PM, arrive ~7:10). The glass-floor Ledge boxes and the sunset from 103 floors up.
  • ~9:15 PM
    🍽️ Uber Eats dinner at the hotel Food
    UberXL back, then Indian delivered — vegetarian + chicken (paneer, dal, chana masala, veg biryani, tandoori chicken, naan).

Cultural Center · Final Walk · Lunch · ORD
  • 8:00–9:00 AM

    Free, and home to the world's largest Tiffany stained-glass dome.
  • 10:15–10:40 AM

    One more look before heading back to the hotel (~10:40).
  • 11:00 AM

    Souvenirs & a short walk Fun
    Garrett Popcorn 📍 (check flavors/ingredients at the store if you're being careful) and a last stroll.

    Back to the Travelodge, restroom, grab the bags.
  • 1:45–2:00 PM
    UberXXL / 2 Ubers → O'Hare (ORD) 📍 Travel
    Allow 45–60 min for the ride and time for check-in/security.
  • 5:18 PM
    ✈️ Delta departs ORD → Albany Travel
    DL 2358 / DL 2660 with a connection in Detroit (DTW).

    Arrive Albany (ALB) 🏡
